# Migrating to 0.5.0

Notes for 0.5.0. The changelog entry links here. A course that builds on 0.4.x keeps building. The release is a minor because five things change what every course's pages look like: section headings, the home page's opening band, the header when a booking link is set, the text selection color, the message icon, the chips in `<assigned-list>` and the separator dots.

## Section headings have no rule

An `h2` in the page body opened with a hairline and a short accent bar. It now opens with space alone.

- Nothing to do. A course that wants the rule back writes it in its own stylesheet for `main :where(h2:not([class]))`.

## The home page band has three panels

`<course-now>` shows **This week** (the week's topics and a link to it), the **next class**, and **Coming up**, side by side while they fit and wrapping one at a time as the page narrows.

- "See the week" moved from the class card to the This week panel. The card keeps "Open the slides".
- Coming up leads each item with its date, in bold, and puts the title under it. The Graded, Test and Reading chips are gone from this card; the schedule page keeps them.
- This week is labelled "Next week" before the Monday of that week's first class, and "First week" before the term.
- The words under each progress bar ("Done", "Now") are still in the page for a screen reader and are no longer shown. The bar's look carries the state.
- New [copy keys](../copy.md#home): `home.weekThis`, `home.weekNext`, `home.weekFirst`, `home.weekWhen`, `home.weekWhenDay`.
- A course that overrides `course-now.webc` takes the kit's copy, or adds the `data-this-week` panel and the `weeks` list in the band's data itself.

## The schedule weeks

- A past week is muted: its text and heading take the muted text color, and its header has no surface or accent rule. The "Done" chip is gone; the word stays in the heading for a screen reader.
- A week's dates sit at the end of its header row, and each badge (Test, Reading check) at the end of its item's row, after the title.
- The column heads (Class, Topics, For this class) start where their columns' content does. They were set in `em` at a smaller size, so they drifted left.
- In the This week card, a day's Today or Next badge sits at the end of its header row, and the week's dates are out of the heading, at the end of its row. The heading is the week alone ("Week 2"); a course that overrides `schedule-weeks.webc` and reads the dates from `#this-week-heading` reads `[data-this-dates]` instead.
- The resources footer is labelled "Resources". The copy key `schedule.weekResources` is renamed `schedule.resources`: a course that overrides it in `strings.json` renames the key there, or the build fails with "not a copy key".
- "Jump to" in `<schedule-overview>` has more space before the month links, and above them when the row wraps.
- Hide past weeks is drawn as a switch: a track that fills with the secondary accent when pressed, beside a muted label. It is the same `button.hide-past[aria-pressed]`; a course that restyled the pill restyles `.hide-past::before`, the track.

## A booking link sits in the header

With `booking` set in `instructor.json`, the header of every page shows the link under the meeting line, and the top menu no longer adds Contact. The footer keeps Contact.

- A course with no `booking` sees no change: the menu still ends with Contact when `instructor.contact` is set.
- A course that wants both removes nothing; add a Contact page to the menu with `eleventyNavigation`.

## Menu groups

A page that other pages name as their `eleventyNavigation.parent` is a group. The header shows its link as a small label with its children under it, in the second column.

```yaml
# content/study.webc
eleventyNavigation:
  key: Study tools
  order: 6

# content/glossary.webc
eleventyNavigation:
  key: Glossary
  parent: Study tools
  order: 2
```

- Optional. A course with no `parent` keys has the menu it had.
- The group page is a real page: write it to list its children. The footer lists a group and its children in one flat list.
- A child with `menu: false` stays out of the header, as a top-level page does.

## Selected text is visible

`::selection` used the surface color, which is the page color, so a selection could not be seen. It is now a wash of the accent.

## The message icon

The "Send me a message" icon in the instructor panel is a speech bubble centered in its circle. Nothing to do.

## Chips in `<assigned-list>` sit at the end of the row

A chip follows its title, at the end of the row, as in the schedule weeks, and every title row is 44px tall, linked or not, so the items are spaced alike.

- The items array needs nothing.
- In the slot form, write the chip after the title (`<dt>Test 1 <span class="due-chip" data-kind="test">Test</span></dt>`) so a screen reader hears the title first. A chip written before the title still shows at the end.

## The separator dot is drawn in CSS

`.separators` (the header's meeting line and eyebrow, the footer, the instructor card, and a deck's eyebrow) drew its dot with the `⏺` character. Safari showed it as a blue emoji button, and screen readers could read it aloud. It is now a small CSS circle in the text color, kept in forced colors.

- Nothing to do. A course that styled `.separators > *::after` restyles a bordered circle, not a glyph.
