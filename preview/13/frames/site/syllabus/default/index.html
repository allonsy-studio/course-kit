Syllabus

Learning outcomes

Upon completing this course students will be able to:

  • Write well-structured HTML pages.
  • Style a page with CSS so it works on a phone and a laptop.
  • Publish a small site and keep it up to date.

Text

Required: None. Every reading is free and online.

  1. Each week's readings are linked from the schedule.

Technology

Bring a laptop meeting the minimum specifications specified by Example College to class every day. You need a reliable internet connection and a free GitHub account. Brightspace access is required.

Coursework

Homework 40%

Weekly practice, posted in your course site and due before the next class.

Tests 40%

Two tests during the term.

Final exam 20%

A short exam in the College's exam period.
