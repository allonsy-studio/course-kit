## Attendance

Come to class. If you cannot, tell the instructor before class when you can.

## Late work

Late work loses ten points a day, up to three days. After that it is not accepted unless you have asked first.

### Illness and emergencies

Ask for an extension as soon as you know you need one. You do not have to give a reason.

## Academic integrity

The work you hand in is your own. Say where an idea or a piece of code came from.
